## Notes for Simplified Chinese (zh-CN)

- The four ECOS category names (Efficient, Clean, Open, Safe) are never translated.
- "Score" is kept in English in all languages as part of "ECOS Score", "Total Score", "Sub-score" etc.
- Technical acronyms (HTTP, SSL, CSP, API, SEO, CDN, etc.) are never translated.
- Brand and product names (Lighthouse, PageSpeed, AbuseIPDB, DynamoDB, etc.) are never translated.
- Percentile labels (p10, p25, p50, p75, p90) are never translated.
- Placeholders ({{domain}}, {{date}}, {{score}}, etc.) are never translated or modified.
- "Performance", "Best Practices", "Benchmark/Benchmarking", "Median", "Audit/Web Audit" are kept in English as they are widely recognized technical terms in Chinese web/tech contexts.
- "Details", "Download", "Badge" are kept in English per glossary convention.
